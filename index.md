## Short biography

I am currently a Research Scientist (Chargé de recherche) at [Inria Grenoble – Rhône-Alpes](https://www.inria.fr/en/centre-inria-grenoble-rhone-alpes), within the [THOTH team](https://team.inria.fr/thoth/). My research focuses on representation learning, optimization, and learning with structure and simulations. I received an ERC Starting Grant in 2026 for the DELPHI project.

Previously, I was a Starting Research Fellow in the same team, working with [Julien Mairal](https://lear.inrialpes.fr/people/mairal/). I completed my PhD in 2021 at the [Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/gatsby/), University College London, under the supervision of [Arthur Gretton](https://www.gatsby.ucl.ac.uk/~gretton/).
