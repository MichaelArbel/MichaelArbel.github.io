I am fortunate to work with great students on rigorous machine learning at the intersection of optimization, statistical learning, and applications. The list below includes students I supervise or co-supervise with close collaborators.

Selected papers below illustrate research carried out with the group, from mathematical guarantees and new optimization methods to scientific inference and imaging.
