## From Basic Machine Learning Models to Advanced Kernel Learning

**2026–2027** · [Course website](https://kernel-learning.github.io/)

Université Grenoble Alpes, Grenoble. Co-taught with Julien Mairal and Scott Pesme.

An introduction to machine learning, from supervised-learning foundations to
advanced kernel methods. The course covers regression, classification,
clustering, dimension reduction, and the theoretical and practical foundations
of kernels.

## Machine Learning with Kernel Methods

**Spring 2026** · [Course website](https://mva-kernel-methods.github.io/course-page/)

Joint IASD, MVA, and MASH master's course, Dauphine–PSL / ENS Paris-Saclay.
Co-taught with Julien Mairal and Jean-Philippe Vert.

The mathematical foundations and design of kernel methods, including
positive-definite kernels, reproducing-kernel
Hilbert spaces, kernel PCA, support vector machines, and kernels for structured
data.
